Executive Summary

About Nivedita

Program & Project Management Leader with proven expertise in driving complex, multi-year, multi-million-dollar delivery initiatives from strategy through execution.

With over two decades in delivery leadership across global technology majors including CGI, Accenture, and Logica, Nivedita combines rigorous governance, financial stewardship, and agile delivery principles to transform ambitious business visions into operational excellence.

Beyond enterprise delivery, Nivedita is a published author and motivational storyteller. Her writing advocates for personal empowerment, women's health awareness, and embracing midlife transformations with courage and purpose.
